About us

We started Pat & Princess Auto Detailing because we love cars and we love seeing people smile when they get them back looking new.

Every job is done by the two of us, not handed off. That means one standard of care, clear communication and no surprises on the bill.

Whether it is a daily commuter, a family van or a weekend show car, we give it the same attention.

How long does a detail take?

An express wash takes about 1 to 2 hours. A full detail takes 4 to 6 hours. Ceramic coating may need 1 to 2 days.

Do you come to me?

Yes, we offer mobile service in our area. We need access to water and power at your location, or you can visit our shop.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, card and mobile payments after the job is done and you are happy.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Tell us right away and we will fix it at no charge.

Can I reschedule?

Of course. Just let us know at least 24 hours before your appointment.
